Jun Xiang

I'm currently a final year joint Phd student at UCSD and SDSU in San Diego, fortunately advised by Prof. Jun Chen and Prof. Sonia Martínez. My research interest is in accelerating the optimization process using machine learning. I am working on stable neural network controllers, reachability analysis, trajectory prediction, and path planning problems.
